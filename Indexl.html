Dear
Prachi,

Some people are called friends. Some become family. And then there are those rare people who quietly become both.

You are not just someone I happen to know. Somewhere between the laughter, the nonsense, the random conversations, the shared moments and all the little things that never make it into photographs, you became family.

We've had moments that made absolutely no sense, memories that probably shouldn't be explained to anyone else, and enough reasons to annoy each other for a lifetime. 😂

But underneath all of that is something simple: I'm genuinely grateful that life gave me a friend who feels like a sister.

So this little website is not meant to be perfect. It's meant to be ours — a tiny collection of moments, smiles and words that say what photographs sometimes cannot.
